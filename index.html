Honest caveats

  • Track B2's "post-cutoff" claim is per-model: models released mid-2026 may have training data extending into the article window; a per-model cutoff table is in progress. Withheld-text controls bound the contamination for every track.
  • Article/filing forward windows overlap in calendar time, so cross-sectional correlation inflates naive significance; treat Track B as a ranking across models on a common sample, not a tradable alpha estimate.
  • Small samples where noted (B2 n<500); error bars matter and seeds are being added.
  • Underlying market and text datasets are licensed; this page publishes aggregates only.
